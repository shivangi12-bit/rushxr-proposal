Our Research

Building from the
Right Foundation.

Before sketching a single concept, we audited the Underneat ecosystem. We looked closely at Kusha Kapila's authentic, unfiltered tone and analyzed your product catalog to find the exact friction points you are solving for Indian women. We distilled this research into the three core pillars that drive our design concepts:

01. Inclusivity

Designing strictly for diverse Indian body types and skin tones.

02. Comfort

Empowering the wearer with breathable, unrestrictive Sensil® technology. The "second skin."

03. The Invisible

"What Are You Wearing Under?" Normalizing the invisible foundation that dictates how the outer layers feel.

Concept 01

The "Peel-Away" Poster Box.

Theme: The Hidden Foundation ("What Are You Wearing Under?")

The Products (The "Stealth Sculpt" Kit)

Strapless Bodysuit & Nipple Covers. Built as the "secret weapon" foundation for tricky event outfits and cut-out dresses.

The Structural Concept & Why This Works

A heavily textured outer sleeve wraps a minimal, skin-toned rigid box. The creator pulls a built-in zipper perforation to physically "strip away" the chaotic outer layer. It physically represents the "What Are You Wearing Under?" hook. Outer clothes can be heavy and complicated, but your foundation (the inner box) is flawless, minimal, and secure.

The Keepsake

The torn outer sleeve unfolds into a heavy-stock typographic poster reading: "Unfold your foundation." Includes a dispenser of invisible fashion tape inscribed with: "The best layers are the ones they never see."

Concept 02

The "Cloud-Pouch" Cylinder.

Theme: True Comfort & Weightlessness

The Products (The "Cloud Base" Kit)

Jelly Bra & Shaping Briefs. A low-friction, ultimate-comfort daily kit that influencers can wear under a basic t-shirt and review immediately.

The Structural Concept & Why This Works

A frosted, semi-translucent vertical tube holding a highly textured, quilted Tyvek "pillow" pouch. Cardboard boxes feel rigid; Tyvek feels like literal air. This design tactilely proves your "second skin" and breathability claims before they even touch the garment.

The Keepsake & Clever Detail

The pouch includes a heavy-duty matte metal carabiner, instantly becoming a durable, water-resistant travel bag. Care instructions are printed directly onto a raw swatch of Sensil® fabric, so they can physically feel the 4-way stretch immediately.

Concept 03

The "Contour Drawer"

Theme: Radical Inclusivity & Diverse Forms

The Products (The "Desi Contour" Kit)

Saree Shapewear & Tummy Tucker Shorts. Targeting the massive Indian festive and wedding wardrobe.

The Structural Concept & Why This Works

A matte-black drawer box. Inside, the garments are flawlessly rolled and separated by thick, premium acrylic dividers. Instead of cliché silhouettes, the dividers are cut into unique, biomorphic curves representing diverse body shapes. It turns "inclusivity" into a sophisticated piece of premium industrial design.

The Keepsake

These dividers lift out to become premium coffee table coasters. The back is subtly inscribed: "Every form is functional. Every shape is valid."
