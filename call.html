Slide 5: The Execution Pipeline

From Blueprint to
Handover.

  • Phase 1: Pre-Production

    • We calculate exact garment volumes to engineer the structural die-lines.

    • We build a "blank" physical mockup to test the tear mechanics (Concept 01) or the tube-fit (Concept 02).

    • We generate photorealistic 3D renders applying your exact brand artwork and typography.

    Client Action: A quick video call to approve the physical blank, followed by final sign-off on the 3D artwork.

  • Phase 2: Production

    • Material Testing: We sample and test different structural fabrics to find the exact option that delivers a premium finish while strictly adhering to your budget.

    • The "Golden Sample": Once the material is locked in, we manufacture exactly one fully finished, perfectly printed physical prototype.

    Client Action: Physical sign-off. You hold the "Golden Sample" and give the green light.

    • Scale: We scale production to your exact requirement, handling the custom machining, fabric stitching, and die-cutting.

  • Phase 3: Post-Production

    • We conduct strict Quality Control on all final assembled materials.

    • We securely freight the pristine, empty kits directly to your warehouse.

    • We supply a visual "Assembly Guide" ensuring your team seamlessly folds the garments and seals the kits perfectly before creator dispatch.

Slide 6: Initial Discovery

Scope &
Logistics.

To ensure our engineering aligns with your physical products and overarching campaign goals, we need to define:

The Objective

What is the primary goal of this PR launch? (e.g., driving viral unboxing moments, highlighting the technical foundation of the garments, or general brand awareness). This will dictate which of the two concepts we lock in.

The Cargo

What specific garments are going into this kit? (We have curated recommendations for both concepts, but we need your final confirmation so we can engineer the exact dimensions).

The Scale

While 100 units is our baseline starting point, the final production run will vary according to your requirements. How many total units are you aiming for, and what is the hard drop date?
