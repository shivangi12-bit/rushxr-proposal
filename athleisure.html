PR Package Concepts.

Slide 1: Concept 01

The Miniature Locker

The Design

A high-end, industrial-style mini gym locker secured by a campaign-dated padlock. It opens to reveal the apparel on miniature hangers alongside curated gym accessories.

Actual Timeline (4–8 Weeks)

Full custom metal fabrication for a flawless, bespoke build.

The "Hack" Timeline (2–3 Weeks)

A "modular and rapid-sourcing" approach.

What We Let Go Of

We bypass custom metal fabrication entirely. Instead of carving custom molds, we repurpose pre-existing items (like metal bento boxes) and finish them with rapid vinyl wraps.

Slide 2: Concept 02

The Articulated Orb

The Design

A futuristic, matte-black streetwear collectible that kinetically "explodes" open via a push-button to unveil a realistic collectible doll alongside vacuum-sealed performance gear.

Actual Timeline (3–4 Months)

Requires custom steel molds and intricate steps for vinyl toys, including 3D sculpting, wax casting, and hand-painting.

The "Hack" Timeline (3–4 Weeks)

High-end rapid fabrication.

What We Let Go Of

Traditional manufacturing and custom vinyl/ABS figures. We swap to SLA resin for the doll, MJF nylon 3D printing for the shell, and laser-cut Delrin for the kinetic mechanism. Paint finishes will be monochromatic to skip tedious hand-painting.

Slide 3: Concept 03

The Foam Roller Vault

The Design

A fully functional, tactical foam roller featuring a hidden internal tube that slides out to present tightly rolled, vacuum-sealed apparel drops.

Actual Timeline (2–3 Months)

Standard manufacturing involving custom steel molds to perfect the polymer shell.

The "Hack" Timeline (2–3 Weeks)

Custom rapid production.

What We Let Go Of

Traditional custom steel molds. We will rely entirely on rapid production materials, such as machined copper and matte black MJF nylon/polymer, to construct the internal sliding sleeve mechanism.

Slide 4: Concept 04

The Weight Plate Vault

The Design

A stack of interlocking Olympic bumper plates that unstack or rotate on a central copper axis to reveal vacuum-sealed clothing hidden in recessed internal cavities.

Actual Timeline (2–3 Months)

Traditional heavy metal casting to achieve authentic weight and finish.

The "Hack" Timeline (2–3 Weeks)

Custom rapid production.

What We Let Go Of

Traditional heavy casting methods. We must rely immediately on rapid production polymers and machined metals to create the recessed internal cavities and the rotating central mechanical axis.
